Thought Over Design · Marketing & Growth Lead · 2024 – 2025

A lead qualification system that closed deals

A brand strategy studio that sold positioning to others had no system for finding and qualifying its own clients.

$100K+in client deals closed
50Kmonthly LinkedIn impressions

The problem

Inbound leads arrived every month with no way to tell a good fit from a poor one, so the team spent time on conversations that were never going to close.

What I did

I designed a data-driven lead qualification system that segmented and filtered monthly inbound leads against the studio's ideal customer profile. I also turned the studio's positioning into sales narratives and proposals, and ran deals from discovery through negotiation.

The result

Better pipeline visibility, faster closes, and two client deals worth $100K+. In parallel I grew the founder's LinkedIn to 50,000 monthly impressions, which became a steady source of new leads.

The Whole Truth · via Thought Over Design · 2022 – 2024

Finding whitespace for a whey protein

A clean-snacks brand was entering a crowded protein category where every pack made the same claims.

100+brands analyzed
30consumer interviews across clients

The problem

Protein shelves all sounded alike. A new entrant needed a position no one else already owned.

What I did

I led a competitive analysis across 100+ brands, mapping their claims, packaging and messaging to find the gaps. It sat alongside the voice-of-customer work I ran across clients, including 30 in-depth consumer interviews on purchase barriers and unmet needs.

The result

The findings informed the packaging and messaging strategy for a product that went on to become the brand's bestseller.

Launching Everyday Protein

A $15M health and wellness brand needed its flagship product to land.

3×monthly order volume

The flagship launch had to work across design, growth and operations at once.

What I did
